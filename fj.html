In memory of Freland Johnson 07/05/1954 to 02/27/2025

Today we celebrate the Life of Captain Freland Johnson. He passed away February 27th at his home in Heron Bay with his family by his side.


Captain Freland is preceded in death by his father Samuel F Johnson; his mother Ova Johnson; His brother Marel Johnson; His sons Jason Hill & Baby Freland A Johnson Jr; His grandson Jonas Freland lyons.


He is survived by his wife Pam; His children Lee, Brandis, Krystal & Scott; His brother Leo (Linda); His sisters Callie (Neil) & Annette (John-Dee); His sister-in-law Dana Johnson; His grandchildren Jesse, Shaun, Caleb, Bryahna, Asher & Autumn; As well as nieces & nephews, other relatives, many friends & his legion family.


A celebration of life will be held on March 22, 2025 from 12-2 at the American legion Post 250 in the Bayley Hall in Fowl River.
